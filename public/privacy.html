Privacy Policy

GZ28 V8 SpeedShop USA LLC · Last updated: November 15, 2026

This policy explains what information GZ28 V8 SpeedShop USA LLC ("GZ28US", "we") handles through its website www.gz28us.com and through "GZ28 Social Link", the internal tool we use to manage our own Facebook Page and Instagram accounts.

What GZ28 Social Link is

GZ28 Social Link is used only by GZ28US staff. It connects to the Facebook Page and Instagram professional accounts that GZ28US owns, so our team can read and answer the messages, comments and mentions those accounts receive, and see their insights, in one place. It is not offered to other businesses or to the public, and nobody outside GZ28US logs in to it.

Information we handle

  • When you contact our accounts on Facebook, Messenger or Instagram: the message, comment or mention you sent, the date, and the public profile name or username that Meta attaches to it.
  • When you fill in one of our lead forms or contact forms: the details you chose to give us, such as name, phone, e-mail and information about your vehicle.
  • When you buy from our shop: the order, shipping and contact details needed to deliver it. Card payments are processed by our payment provider; we do not store card numbers.

How we use it

  • To answer you and follow up on what you asked.
  • To prepare quotes, orders and services you requested.
  • To keep a record of our conversations with customers.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use the content of your messages for advertising.

Who we share it with

Only with service providers that run our systems (hosting, database, payment and messaging platforms), and only as needed to operate them, or when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep conversations and customer records for as long as they are needed for the purposes above and for our legal and accounting obligations.

Your choices and data deletion

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Send your request to gz28us@hotmail.com and tell us the name or username you used to contact us. We will delete the information we are not legally required to keep and confirm when it is done. Messages you sent us on Facebook or Instagram can also be removed by you on those platforms.
